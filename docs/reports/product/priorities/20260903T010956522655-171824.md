The operator states a standing priority in an addendum comment on issue
#3245, directly relevant to how verification work on this repo should be
done generally, not just for this issue:

**Verify a diagnosis by reproducing it, not by re-reading it.** PR #3251
recorded a root-cause claim ("CLI does not fire plugin hooks headless")
that read as well-cited and internally consistent, but was wrong --
found only because the operator independently cross-checked it against
other sessions' logs and asked a verification session to reproduce the
failure rather than the report. The operator's own framing: "If the
diagnosis is wrong and gets recorded, the next session reads it as
established fact and stops looking. If it is right in some narrower
form, the exact boundary is what future work needs." This is a durable
standard for `independent-verification-*` roles specifically: matching a
subject record's citations to real file:line content is necessary but
not sufficient when the record's central claim is a causal/diagnostic
one -- that claim needs its own reproduction, with the command and raw
output, not just confirmation that the citations exist.
