   `all_scope=True` into `roster_watchdog()`, reusing the existing `--all`
   flag.
7. Acceptance — `tests/test_spawn.py` gains a two-session-scoping case
   covering own-entry filtering and orphan surfacing.
8. Acceptance — single-session/unset-env machine behaves identically to
   pre-change behavior (empty-state parity).
